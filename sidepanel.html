    PRIVACY

    What BrowserCrew keeps

    Local first
    🧾

    Job history: the job goal, status, result, and evidence are saved on this device so interrupted work is not lost.

    🔖

    Reusable jobs: skill names and instructions are saved on this device until you delete them in Memory.

    🔑

    AI secret key: kept only for the current Chrome session. It is not written into job history or reusable jobs.

    📄

    Page text: used for the active job. The raw page-text snapshot is not copied into saved job history by this workflow.

    SITE ACCESS

    BrowserCrew asks before reading a new site

    When you choose a page, Chrome asks whether BrowserCrew may read that site. Saying no leaves the page untouched.

    💡 Example: giving access to shop.example.com does not automatically give BrowserCrew access to a different website.
